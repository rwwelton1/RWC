The circumstances were, by all accounts, quite ordinary — a bustling day at the Keeneland racecourse, the air thick with anticipation. Where We Gather

Tate House

2973 GA-53, Tate, Georgia 30177

A mansion that never felt imported or imposed — like it had simply risen out of the Georgia hills on its own.

Colonel Sam Tate raised this house in 1926 from the pink marble of his own quarries — the same stone used in the Lincoln Memorial and the U.S. Supreme Court. Pink Georgia marble reads less as pink than as warm: nearly ivory in morning light, deepening to a burnished rose by afternoon.

The grounds spread across 27 acres framed by the Appalachian foothills, with English gardens terracing down from the house and a vine-draped conservatory connecting the private quarters to the formal rooms.

While You're in Canton

Both hotels sit minutes from a walkable downtown. A few places we love, if you're making a weekend of it.

Downtown Kitchen is the special-occasion room downtown — refined, romantic, live music some nights. Local on North is the reliable all-rounder, upscale but relaxed. Riverstone Corner Bistro has rooftop seating and easy parking, which downtown doesn't always. For something louder, B & B Social pairs Southern plates with duckpin bowling and arcade games. Most downtown spots close Sundays, so plan Friday or Saturday.

Reformation Brewery is where we're hosting the Friday welcome party — worth a look earlier in the weekend too, with a big taproom and cornhole out back. The Holler is the beer-focused spot downtown with live music and outdoor seating. Laurel Canyon Brewing is smaller and quieter, and Green Line Brewery is a genuine hole-in-the-wall nano brewery if that's your thing.

Bizarre Coffee is the downtown standby — outdoor seating, seasonal menu, opens at 7. Stitch Coffee is the family-friendly option with a kids' menu. For a proper Saturday breakfast, Keithsburg Cafe is the local favourite — unassuming, quick, and reasonably priced — and Eggs Up Grill opens at 6 if you're up early.

Main Street is small and easily walked in an afternoon. The White Cottage on Main and Boundless Boutique are both worth a stop, and B. Loved is a few doors along. Hours vary and several close Sundays, so late morning Saturday is the safest window.

The dress code is formal — floor-length gowns, elegant cocktail dresses, or suits and tuxedos. Parts of the celebration are outdoors on a lawn, so keep that in mind when choosing footwear.

Children under 13 are not permitted at the Saturday wedding venue, but they're warmly welcome at the Friday night welcome party. We hope that lets families celebrate with us while keeping the big day an elegant evening.

Tate House has paved pathways and accessible entrances throughout the estate. For specific accessibility needs, email us at WeltonWedding1@gmail.com and we'll do everything we can to accommodate you.

Yes. On Friday evening a shuttle runs to and from Reformation Brewery, where the welcome party begins at 8:30 PM. On Saturday, shuttles run to and from Tate House throughout the day. Pickup locations and departure times will be shared closer to the date.

We're having an unplugged ceremony and kindly ask that phones and cameras be put away so everyone can be fully present. Our photographer will capture every moment. Feel free to take photos during cocktail hour and the reception.
